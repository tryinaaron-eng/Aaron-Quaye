Our Bespoke Services

At AQ Signature Weddings, we believe every event should be as unique as the person it’s for. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, we provide end-to-end planning excellence.

Full Wedding Planning

This is our most popular service. We walk with you from the very first day until the last guest leaves. This includes:

  • Venue sourcing and site visits.
  • Vendor selection and contract negotiations.
  • Budget management and tracking.
  • Detailed wedding day itinerary and coordination.

Corporate Galas & Events

Impress your clients and celebrate your employees with a professionally managed corporate event. We specialize in:

  • Annual Award ceremonies.
  • Product launches and branding events.
  • Holiday parties and team-building retreats.
  • Logistics, lighting, and audio-visual setups.

Creative Decor & Styling

If you have the planning under control but need an expert eye for the "look," this is for you. We focus on:

  • Floral design and centerpiece arrangements.
  • Theme development and color palette selection.
  • Table setting and linen styling.
  • Atmospheric lighting design.
